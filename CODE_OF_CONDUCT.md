# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CrewForge and its community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its participants.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub contact channel. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
